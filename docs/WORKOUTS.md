# Workout planner

The workout planner lives in its own module and shares the user's profile with
the food planner (sex, weight, health conditions, diet preference, food goal).

```
src/lib/workouts/        pure, unit-tested logic (tests/workouts.test.ts)
  types.ts               locations, levels, goals, equipment, muscles, roles, patterns, Exercise, Program
  exercises.ts           85-exercise library
  generator.ts           4-week program generator, exercise selection, swaps
  progress.ts            sequential progress replay: calendar, streak, missed days
  load.ts                MET energy, starting loads, progressive-overload rules, e1RM
  fuel.ts                food-planner link: calorie add-back, training-day protein, Indian fuel ideas
  format.ts              dose / time formatting
src/server/services/workouts.ts   DB access, safety flags, sessions, training-day targets, chart data
src/server/actions/workouts.ts    setup, swaps, start / log / finish sessions
src/app/(app)/workouts/           hub, setup, day preview, exercise library
src/app/(focus)/workouts/session/ full-screen session player
src/components/workouts/          figure animation, media slot, calendar, swap sheet, session player
```

Sample programs generated by the engine: [WORKOUT_SAMPLES.md](WORKOUT_SAMPLES.md)
(`npx tsx scripts/workout-samples.ts` regenerates it).

## Setup questions

1. Where will you train? Home · Gym · Mix
2. Level: Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced
3. Goal: Weight loss · Muscle gain · Stay fit — pre-filled from the food plan (losing weight → weight loss, otherwise stay fit)
4. Days per week: 3 · 4 · 5 · 6
5. Home equipment: none, dumbbells, resistance bands, pull-up bar (home and mix only)

A doctor note must be acknowledged before a plan is built. The food profile adds
condition-specific cautions and selection rules:

| Profile flag | Effect |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy | No jumping (`impact: "high"`), no exercises lying on the back (`supine`) |
| Heart disease, hypertension, age 65+ | No jumping |
| Under 18 | Caution shown: supervision, technique over heavy weights |
| Kidney disease, under 18 | Training-day protein is never raised |

## Program structure

- **28 days** (4 weeks × 7) in a fixed weekly rhythm (W = workout, A = active recovery, R = rest):
  3 days `W A W R W A R` · 4 days `W W R W W A R` · 5 days `W W W A W W R` · 6 days `W W W W W W R`.
- **Splits**
  - Home: full body A/B/C (3 days), or lower / upper / full / cardio-circuit splits (4–6 days).
  - Gym: full body (3 days, beginner and intermediate), upper / lower (4 days), upper / lower + push / pull / legs (5 days, intermediate+), push / pull / legs ×2 (6 days, intermediate+). Advanced 3-day uses PPL. Beginners at 5–6 days get upper / lower plus cardio-and-core days.
  - Mix: gym strength days alternating with home circuits.
- **Exercise selection** fills role slots (squat, hinge, push, pull, core, conditioning…) with the best match for setting, equipment, level and safety flags:
  - Home plans are bodyweight-first; equipment versions are offered through swaps.
  - Gym plans prefer loadable machine and free-weight lifts. Beginners favour machines and dumbbells; others favour barbells.
  - A repeated focus in the same week (e.g. a second upper day) rotates to the next-best exercise.
- **Day 1 is a gentle intro**: one fewer set (minimum 2), 2 fewer reps or 15% lighter loads, longer rests, no finisher.
- **Progression across weeks**
  - Bodyweight: reps +0 / +2 / +2 / +4, with an extra set on main lifts from week 3.
  - Timed holds and finishers: longer each week.
  - Harder variations from week 3, never adding jumping. Examples: incline push-up → push-up, chair squat → bodyweight squat.
  - Gym: planned loads +5% a week, with an extra set on main lifts from week 3.
  - Weight-loss plans shorten rests from week 3.
- **Session length**: home ≈ 20–40 min (caps 32 / 38 / 42 min by level), gym ≤ ~50–68 min. Accessory sets are trimmed first if a session runs long.
- Every workout has a warm-up (march or bike, arm and hip circles, cat–cow or world's-greatest stretch, plus squats or inchworms) and a cool-down (3–4 stretches).

## Days continue where you stopped (no reset)

A program is a **sequence** of days, not fixed dates (`replayProgram` in `progress.ts`).
Progress is derived by replaying completed sessions from the start date:

- A **workout day waits**. If it is missed, the next calendar day shows the same workout; nothing is skipped and nothing resets.
- **Rest and active-recovery days pass** with the calendar on their own (recovery can also be done and logged).
- **Training through a rest day** ("Train anyway") is allowed only across rest / recovery days, never past a workout.
- **Streak** = consecutive calendar days on plan; rest days count, a missed workout ends it. The day's own pending workout doesn't break it.
- The weekly calendar shows completed, missed, today, upcoming and rest. Each status has an icon and a text label, not just a colour.

## Session

- Elapsed timer (pausable; counts only while the page is visible).
- Animated demonstration, steps and common mistakes.
- Per-set reps and weight entry with checkboxes; timed sets with a countdown.
- Rest countdown with +15 s / skip, chime and vibration.
- Next-exercise preview and screen wake lock.
- Sets are saved as they are ticked; reopening the session resumes at the first unfinished exercise.
- **Overload suggestions** (`suggestOverload`, double progression):
  - Every planned set at the top of the rep range → add the smallest practical increment: barbell +2.5 kg upper / +5 kg lower, dumbbells +1–2 kg, machines +2.5 / +5 kg, kettlebells +4 kg.
  - Most sets below the bottom of the range → step back one increment.
  - Otherwise hold the weight and aim for one more rep.
  - Bodyweight moves → +2 reps, or a harder variation once the range tops out at 15+.
  - The suggested weight pre-fills the next session.
- **Starting loads** (`suggestLoad`) are deliberately conservative:
  - Formula: exercise ratio × body weight (capped at 90 kg) × level factor (1 / 1.35 / 1.7) × sex factor (women 0.6 upper body, 0.75 lower body).
  - Rounded to practical plates and dumbbells, with the empty bar as the barbell minimum.

## Energy

`kcal = MET × 3.5 × body weight (kg) ÷ 200 × minutes` (ACSM), with METs from the Compendium
of Physical Activities. Working time uses the exercise MET. Rest between sets uses
`min(MET, 3.0)`, because people stand, walk and set up between sets. The shown figure is
gross energy. **Net** energy (MET − 1, i.e. excluding resting metabolism already counted
in the daily target) is what feeds the food plan. After a session, the estimate is scaled
to the real elapsed time (within ±50%).

## Food-planner link

`trainingDayTargets` (`fuel.ts`), applied by `trainingContext` (`services/workouts.ts`):

| | Calories | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| Weight loss | + 50% of net workout kcal (deficit kept) | ≥ 1.5 g/kg reference weight on training days |
| Muscle gain | + 100% of net workout kcal, + 150 kcal lean-gain surplus on training days unless the food plan has a weight-loss target (then it's a recomposition: deficit kept) | ≥ 1.8 g/kg every day |
| Stay fit | + 100% of net workout kcal | ≥ 1.5 g/kg on training days |

- The add-back is capped at **600 kcal/day**.
- Reference weight is the weight at BMI 25 for people above it, as in the food plan.
- Extra protein replaces energy from carbohydrate. On keto and low-carb plans the added energy goes to fat instead, so the net-carb limit is untouched.
- Home shows the workout bonus on the calorie ring; the meal planner shows a training-day banner.
- **Pre/post-workout ideas** come from Indian and South Indian food and are filtered by diet and keto: idli with sambar, ragi malt, poha, moong sprouts chaat, egg bhurji, boiled eggs, paneer tikka, curd rice, buttermilk with roasted chana, chicken tikka, tofu or soya.

## Media

Each exercise has a `media` slot (`{ kind: "image" | "video", src, credit }`). When it is empty,
the app draws an animated SVG figure for the exercise's movement pattern (20 patterns, key
poses morphed with SMIL). With `prefers-reduced-motion`, the figure shows the key position
without moving.

## Data model

- `workout_profiles` — the setup answers and when the doctor note was acknowledged.
- `workout_programs` — a snapshot of the 28 generated days (JSON), swaps, start date and status.
- `workout_sessions` — one per program day (unique). Fields: status, date, duration, kcal, net kcal.
- `workout_sets` — per set: reps, seconds, weight, done.

Progress, streaks and the calendar are always derived from completed sessions, never stored.
